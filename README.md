An interactive universe built for BST Toolchain Team's 10th anniversary — using a star field to describe engineering, and AI to imagine the future.

A chip is a small universe. Accuracy, Performance, Memory, Environment — four galaxies in tension. Every star is a real engineering dimension. Our work is to keep approaching a global optimum that never truly exists, within every customer deadline.

This world was conceived by engineers and co-authored with AI: the narrative, the knowledge galaxy, the story of 2030, the dialogue with a future toolchain — all shaped by human intuition and AI working together.

Inspired by BTS's *Mikrokosmos*, the theme song accompanies the full journey — six chapters, thirty-two planets, and a message written to your future self.

**The radius of exploration can always go further.**

## Project Introduction

BST Mikrokosmos is an AI-generated interactive web experience that reimagines the world of AI Compiler and Toolchain engineering as a living universe.

In our daily work, we constantly balance performance, memory, accuracy, maintainability, hardware constraints, customer deadlines, and countless optimization objectives. Every technical decision becomes a trade-off among multiple dimensions.

This project transforms those abstract engineering challenges into a navigable Chip Universe:

- Galaxies represent major optimization domains.

- Planets represent individual optimization variables.

- Coordinates represent possible futures.

Through AI-assisted design and storytelling, BST Mikrokosmos explores a question:

**What if engineering itself were a form of cosmic exploration?**

---

## Inspiration

Modern AI systems are becoming increasingly complex.

As engineers, we rarely search for a perfect solution.

Instead, we continuously move through a vast high-dimensional space, attempting to approach a better answer within limited time and resources.

The idea behind BST Mikrokosmos emerged from this realization:

- Every optimization target is a dimension.

- Every engineering decision is a coordinate.

- Every coordinate leads to a different future.»

The project reinterprets AI Compiler development as an interstellar journey through an evolving universe.

---

## Core Concept

From Optimization Space to Chip Universe

Traditional engineering diagrams often describe systems through metrics and charts.

BST Mikrokosmos takes a different approach.

Instead of showing optimization objectives as numbers, it visualizes them as celestial bodies within a universe.

- Galaxies → Major optimization domains
- Planets → Individual optimization variables
- Routes → Engineering decisions
- Exploration → Continuous optimization
- Human × AI → Collaborative navigation

This universe is intentionally open-ended.

There is no final destination.

There is only continuous exploration.

---

## Human × AI

AI is not portrayed as a replacement for engineers.

Instead, AI acts as a navigator.

Humans define direction.

AI expands the search radius.

Together, they explore larger portions of the solution space than either could alone.

The future envisioned by this project is not Human versus AI.

It is Human × AI.
